FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? hello@whatstheirpay.com

Is this the person's actual salary?

No. It's a market estimate for someone with that title, level, employer type and location. We never see payroll data, and every estimate is labeled that way.

How accurate is it?

Every estimate comes with a confidence score and a range, and the range widens when we know less. A specific title, a known company and a city usually give a high-confidence range.

We'd rather show an honest wide range than a precise-looking wrong number.

Where do the numbers come from?

An open model: a benchmark base salary for each of 45+ role families, adjusted for level, employer type, market and experience. Benchmarks are set from public sources such as government wage statistics, H-1B salary disclosures and pay ranges in job posts.

Every assumption is listed in our methodology.

What does the extension read?

Only the profile or job you're viewing, and only after you click. It sends the title, company, location and experience dates to calculate the estimate.

No browsing history, messages or contacts. It doesn't crawl LinkedIn or run in the background.

Is it affiliated with LinkedIn?

No. WhatsTheirPay is an independent tool that works on pages you already have open. LinkedIn is a trademark of LinkedIn Corporation.

What counts as an estimate?

One unique profile, job or role. Viewing it again within 30 days is free, and so are up to 10 edits to its details. Examples on this page never count.

Can I opt out my own profile?

Yes. and we'll stop estimating it and delete any stored lookups of it.

How do I cancel?

From your account page, in one click. A 30-day pass simply ends; it never renews.
