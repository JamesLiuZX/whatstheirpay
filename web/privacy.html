Privacy

Privacy, in plain words

What the extension reads

Only the LinkedIn profile or job you're viewing, and only after you click the pay chip (or, if you turned on auto-reveal, when the page opens). It reads the name, headline, current title and company, location and experience dates shown on the page. It never reads messages, contacts or your browsing history, and it never browses LinkedIn on its own.

What we store

  • Guests: a salted hash of your IP address and an identifier for each profile you estimated, to enforce the 3-a-day limit. No names, URLs or results.
  • Signed-in users: your email, your plan, and your lookup history (the inputs and the estimate) so you can revisit and export it.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We keep the Stripe customer and subscription IDs, never card details.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, run ad trackers, or build profiles of the people you look up. Estimates are generated from role-level market data, not from anyone's actual pay.

Opting out your profile

Anyone can ask us to stop estimating their profile from the opt-out form on the home page. We honor it immediately for every user and delete stored lookups of that profile.
